SPRINT · 04

Computing program

Energy-balance models today. GPU simulation of plasma-jet implosions and neutron transport next.

Now

Energy-balance models.

The first stage is simple energy-balance models on an ordinary computer. They do not simulate plasma; they check the concept for consistency: whether there is enough energy for the exhaust, where the rest goes, and which parameters are critical. These models will serve as checkpoints for the more complex simulations.

Next stage · GPU

From single simulations to statistical ensembles.

A single run says nothing about robustness: we need hundreds of variants with scattered initial conditions. That takes GPUs.

TaskMethodWhat it gives
Plasma-jet ensemblesParticle-in-cell and hydrodynamics on GPUs, for example WarpXHow spread in jet speed and timing affects liner symmetry
Neutron transportMonte Carlo, for example OpenMCHow much neutron energy the liner, shield and coils absorb
Surrogate modelsNeural networks trained on the ensemble dataFast scans of operating modes instead of long runs for every point
